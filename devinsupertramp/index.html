The story

Devin Graham, a.k.a. DevinSuperTramp, built one of YouTube’s biggest adventure channels, cinematic stunt films, video games in real life, and brand campaigns shot on cinema cameras. I joined in August 2014 and stayed until May 2019. In that time the channel grew past six million subscribers and a billion views, signed with Whistle Sports (2018), and shot brand work for Pepsi, Nikon, LEGO, Universal, Subaru, and LG.

It was a small team and everyone did everything. We worked on every type of production, from shorts to feature-length films.

All the hats

A five-person production company doesn’t have the luxury of one job title. These are some of the roles I had at Devin Super Tramp:

Social Media & Content

I ran the social strategy across platforms, 10 million followers in three years, a Facebook page built from zero to 3 million, all organic.

Video & Photography Editor

I edited main-channel videos and behind-the-scenes, in Adobe Premiere Pro.

1st & 2nd Assistant Camera

I pulled focus and built cameras on RED cinema cameras, shooting up to 8K.

Set Design

I built sets for productions including the LEGO Battle.

Sound & Lighting

I helped with sound recording and lighting on location shoots.

Copyright Manager

I managed the company’s copyright claims and content rights.

Documentary Post-Production

I did post-production on the feature-length documentary Transcend: The Jon Mozo Story , in the Adobe Creative Cloud Suite.

Operations & Studio

I ran operations for the production company, including a remodel of the Devin Super Tramp studio.

Cameraman

Highlights

Transcend: The Jon Mozo Story

I worked on the post-production for the company’s feature-length documentary, Transcend: The Jon Mozo Story , six years in the making, about surfer Jon Mozo, who lived with cystic fibrosis. It premiered at the Honolulu International Film Festival on November 4, 2016.

Gaten Matarazzo & CCD Smiles

In November 2017, Devin Super Tramp made Stranger Things Meets E.T. In Real Life Parody! , guest-starring Matt Meese and Stacey Harkey from Studio C, and featuring a cameo from Gaten Matarazzo, the actor who plays Dustin on Netflix’s Stranger Things . Gaten was in Utah for CCD Smiles, the nonprofit supporting people with cleidocranial dysplasia (CCD), the rare condition he lives with; the video promoted the charity.

The brand era

Between the viral films, the company shot brand work: Nikon (a drifting tank through a mall, shot on the Z 6), LEGO and Nickelodeon (a Ninjago battle for the Kids’ Choice Awards), Nintendo (Fortnite in real life), Ubisoft (nine projects), Universal (a Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom promo), Subaru, LG, Ford, Disney, and Pepsi.
