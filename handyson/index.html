Questions

Are you licensed electricians or plumbers?

No. HandySon does small jobs that do not need a license. If your job needs a licensed pro, we will tell you honestly and help you find one.

What if the job turns out bigger than expected?

We stop and explain. We agree a new price before going on. You never get a surprise bill.

Do I need to buy parts myself?

Either way works. We can bring common parts, or you can buy them yourself. Parts are charged at cost — we agree it beforehand.

How do I pay?

We will agree how you would like to pay when we agree the price.

What areas do you cover?

Washington, DC, Prince George’s County, and Montgomery County. If you are just outside, ask in the form — we will tell you honestly if we can come.
