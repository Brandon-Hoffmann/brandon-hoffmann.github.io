What we clean

Standard Clean

Best for regular upkeep and recurring visits.

  • Kitchen counters, sink, and appliance exteriors
  • Bathrooms , toilets, tubs, showers, sinks, and mirrors
  • Dusting, floors vacuumed and mopped throughout
  • Bedrooms and living areas tidied and dusted
  • Trash emptied

Deep Clean

Best for a first visit, spring cleaning, or a home that needs extra attention.

  • Everything in a Standard Clean, plus:
  • Baseboards, doors, and light switches wiped
  • Inside microwave, and extra detail in kitchen and bathrooms
  • Ceiling fans and vents dusted
  • Extra time on built-up areas
