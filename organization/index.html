What I organize

Closets & Bedrooms

Best for a closet that has outgrown its system, or a bedroom that needs a reset.

  • Sort, edit, and put every item in its place
  • Clothing grouped so getting dressed is easy
  • Shelves, drawers, and hanging space used well
  • Donation bags bagged and ready to go

Kitchens & Pantries

Best for a kitchen where finding things takes longer than cooking.

  • Pantry sorted, grouped, and labeled
  • Cabinets and drawers set up around how you cook
  • Expired and duplicate items cleared out
  • Counter space freed up for daily use

Home Offices

Best for a desk buried under paper, or a spare room doing too many jobs.

  • Paper sorted into keep, file, and shred
  • Desk set up for the work you actually do
  • Supplies and files given a home
  • Cables and clutter cleared from the workspace

Also available: garages, basements, moving and unpacking, and whole-home projects. Ask in the form below.

Questions

How is pricing set?

By room size, at a flat rate. Prices start at $350 for a small room, and you confirm the price before the appointment.

Do I need to be home?

Yes, for the first part at least. Organizing is about your stuff and your habits, so we decide together what stays and what goes.

Do you bring bins and labels?

We talk through supplies before we start. You can buy what your space needs, or I can pick items up and add the cost to your booking.

What if I need to reschedule?

Text me as early as you can and we will find a new time.

How do I pay?

We will confirm payment options by text when your booking is confirmed.
